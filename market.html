Approve the marketplace once, then list any eye you own. Cancel any time before a sale and your eye returns to you.

How it works

  • List: approve the marketplace, set a price, and your eye moves into escrow.
  • Buy: pay the exact listing price. The eye transfers to you the moment payment lands — 95% to the seller, 5% royalty to the creator wallet.
  • Cancel / reprice: only the seller can cancel a listing or change its price, any time before it sells.
  • Fees: 0% platform fee. The only costs are the BNB gas for your own transactions.
